# ANotes: offline voice notes

ANotes turns your voice into text on the phone itself, with no internet and no account.
Notes and recordings stay on your phone, as plain Markdown.

- Website: https://getanotes.com
- What's new: https://getanotes.com/changelog.html
- Privacy: https://getanotes.com/privacy/

## What it does
- Dictation that works in airplane mode, with free speech models you download once.
- Every recording stays on its note: play it, share it, transcribe it again.
- Reads your notes aloud and keeps your place.
- Folders, search, archive and a trash with Undo. Share a note as text or as a .md file.

## About this repository
This repository is the website getanotes.com. The app's code is not here.
To report a problem, use the form on the website, or Help and support in the app.
