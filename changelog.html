What's new.

Every release of the ANotes alpha, newest first, in plain words: what you will notice, not what we rewrote.

In testingnow

AI that runs on the phone

  • Tidy a dictation, make a checklist, sum up or translate a note, with the network off.
  • On our test phones now. It ships when every check passes.
1.6219 October 2026

Bookmarks, and a promise: nothing is lost

  • Bookmark a sentence while a note reads aloud, and come back to it days later.
  • One Bookmarks page for every note: rename, delete or share them.
  • Saving never deletes a note, and Undo puts back only what it changed. We spent a week checking every way a note could be lost before adding anything new.
1.6113–18 October 2026

Read aloud keeps your place

  • The sentence being read is marked, and the page follows it. Pause, leave, and carry on from the same spot.
  • Dictation works with Bluetooth headphones, even ones you connect mid-recording.
  • Name a note or a folder by voice.
  • Fast note opens straight into the note, ready for your voice or your keyboard.
1.6013 October 2026

Formatting and sharing

  • Headings, bold, lists and checklists from a bar on the keyboard.
  • Share a note as text, as its recordings or as a .md file.
  • Text shared from any app becomes a note.
1.5910 October 2026

One way to do things

  • Every menu works the same way, and the player keeps its buttons together.
1.589 October 2026

Faster to move around

  • Swipe right on a note's top bar to go back.
  • The Trash has its place in Storage.
1.567 October 2026

Dictation learns your words

  • My words: teach dictation the names and terms you use.
  • Details on every note: its folder, dates, words and recordings.
  • Search finds notes in the Archive and the Trash too.
1.514 October 2026

Folders and recordings, kept whole

  • A folder goes to the Trash whole, and comes back whole.
  • A note can hold more than one recording.
