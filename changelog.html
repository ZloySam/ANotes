What's new.

One update a week, newest first, in plain words: what you will notice, not what we rewrote. Items marked In testing are on our test phones and reach you when every check passes.

26 Oct – 1 NovVersions 1.67–1.72

Nothing gets lost, even in a crash

In testing

  • A recording cut off when the app closes, from a crash, a swipe away or a restart, comes back with its note. All of it.
  • Undo after every move: into a folder, out of the Archive, back from the Trash.
  • A Fold button on the keyboard bar tucks the original text behind one line, after the AI rewrites it.
  • Notes breathe a little: more room between lines, and space above each heading.
  • Read aloud uses your phone's own voice by default, with nothing extra to download.
  • On first start, a short guided setup for the AI, or Later.
  • What's new and the open-source licences now live in Settings › About.
19 – 25 OctVersions 1.62–1.66

Bookmarks, and AI that runs on the phone

  • Bookmark a sentence while a note reads aloud, and come back to it days later.
  • One Bookmarks page for every note: rename, delete or share them.
  • Saving never deletes a note, and Undo puts back only what it changed. We spent a week checking every way a note could be lost before adding anything new.

In testing

  • AI on the phone, with the network off: tidy a dictation, make a checklist, sum up, translate, give a title or draft a message.
  • Every AI change is one step with Undo. Clean up won't replace your text if it would drop a word.
  • Long recordings are summed up in one go, and a talk or lecture gets its own Talk notes.
  • Ask a question about a note, and keep the chat as a note of its own.
12 – 18 OctVersions 1.60–1.61

Formatting, sharing, and read aloud that keeps your place

  • Headings, bold, lists and checklists from a bar on the keyboard.
  • Share a note as text, as its recordings or as a .md file. Text shared from any app becomes a note.
  • The sentence being read is marked, and the page follows it. Pause, leave, and carry on from the same spot.
  • Dictation works with Bluetooth headphones, even ones you connect mid-recording.
  • Name a note or a folder by voice.
5 – 11 OctVersions 1.56–1.59

Dictation learns your words

  • My words: teach dictation the names and terms you use, and every new transcription is corrected.
  • Details on every note: its folder, dates, words and recordings.
  • Search finds notes in the Archive and the Trash too.
  • Swipe right on a note's top bar to go back.
  • Every menu works the same way.
1 – 4 OctVersions 1.49–1.54

Faster to record, safer to tidy

  • The mic records the moment you tap it, and long recordings are written while you talk.
  • Read aloud starts sooner.
  • A folder goes to the Trash whole, and comes back whole.
  • A note can hold more than one recording.
  • A recording with no speech in it says so, with Transcribe again.
