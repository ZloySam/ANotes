An architecture, not a promise.

Your notes never leave the phone, because nothing in the app has a way to send them anywhere: no account, no sync, no analytics. They live in ANotes' private storage, which other apps can't open, protected by your phone's own encryption and screen lock. When you want a note somewhere else, you share it yourself.

Six things ANotes does best.

Dictation
Talk. It types, offline.
  • Tap and speak. The text is written on the phone.
  • Long recordings are written while you talk.
  • Leave mid-thought: your words still arrive.
  • Teach it names and terms with My words.
Read aloud
Listen to any note.
  • Tap and the note reads itself, with on-device voices.
  • Start at the cursor, or only the part you selected.
  • The sentence being read is marked, and the page follows it.
Your recordings
Your voice, kept. Your rules.
  • The real audio stays next to the text. A note can hold several.
  • Transcribe again with a better model. What you typed is never replaced.
  • Keep audio forever, or let it clear itself after a set time.
Fast note
An idea? One tap.
  • Tap on your home screen and just talk.
  • It's saved before the thought slips away.
  • Prefer typing? There is a text widget too.
Languages
Speaks your languages.
  • Pick dictation and read-aloud models from the built-in library.
  • Only the languages you need. Nothing is preinstalled.
  • Download once, then it works offline.
Write & share
Make it neat. Send it anywhere.
  • Headings, bold, lists and checklists right on the keyboard.
  • Share to messengers, email or AI apps: as text, audio or a .md file.
  • Text shared from any app becomes a note.

Why offline matters.

The problem ANotes is actually solving.

01
You talk faster than you type.

Dictate a note and get text back, with nothing sent off the phone to produce it.

02
Note apps have burned you before.

Every note is plain Markdown, the same format Obsidian uses, not a locked format of our own. Send any note to Obsidian, or anywhere else, with Share.

03
Most voice apps send your voice to their servers.

Once it's there, you can't check whether it's kept or used to train their AI. And on a weak signal, cloud dictation stalls. ANotes doesn't need a signal at all.

04
Transcripts get words wrong.

The original recording is kept next to the text, so you can hear what you actually said, and transcribe it again as better models arrive.

What happens to your email
  • It's used only to answer you and, if you ticked the box, to send one message when ANotes is ready. No newsletter; never sold or shared.
  • It's stored on a private server run by the developer, never in a code repository. A copy goes to the developer's private Telegram chat so it's seen quickly.
  • Your IP address is used only in memory, to limit how often the form can be sent. It isn't saved with your message.
  • The spam check is Cloudflare Turnstile, which processes some technical data from your browser (Cloudflare's privacy policy).
  • Release-notice addresses are deleted once that message is sent; messages when the conversation is over, and after 12 months at the latest. Encrypted server backups age out within 6 months.
  • To see or delete what's stored about you, send the form again from the same address and say so.
